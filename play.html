THROUGH

You slip past the core and out beyond the grid's edge. The Lattice falls quiet behind you. For the first time, no one is chasing. You're free.

HOW TO PLAY

You are the bright node. Move with the mouse or a finger-drag. The hunter-nodes home in on you — touching one, or the glowing web strung between two nearby nodes, gets you caught.

Node personalities

  • Purple — Chaser. Straight, aggressive pursuit.
  • Pink — Ambusher. Aims ahead of where you're heading.
  • Cyan — Erratic. Wobbles and orbits around you.
  • Orange — Shy. Chases, but bolts when it gets close.
  • Blue — Scatter. Roams the arena, only loosely after you.
  • Yellow — Cluster. Slow, weak chase, sticks tight to its kind.

Same-colour nodes flock into groups, and the webs between nearby nodes are lethal. The longer you survive, the tighter the groups pack and the bigger the nodes grow.

Powerups & threats

  • ◆Gem — points. Grab several in quick succession for a ×combo multiplier.
  • ★Star — a blast wave that destroys every node it reaches.
  • ❄Freeze — freezes all nodes for a few seconds (their webs still kill).
  • ⬡Shield — absorbs one hit and shoves the swarm away.
  • »Shooter — auto-fires darts in your direction of travel for a few seconds, destroying nodes.
  • ◉Boss — the big spiked eye. It takes on the personality of its colour. Catch it in a star-blast to pop it into a swarm of its own colour (+50).
